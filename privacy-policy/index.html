Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026.

What we collect

The only information this website collects is what you enter into the consultation form: your name, dealership, email address, phone number, the segment you operate in and any message you choose to add.

Why we collect it

We use it to contact you about the consultation you requested and to prepare for that conversation. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we are in contact with you about your dealership, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our business records. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Analytics and cookies

We may use standard web analytics to understand which pages are read and how visitors arrive. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Your choices
