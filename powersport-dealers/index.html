AMDS Inventory Advantage

A proprietary system pushes inventory to Google and social targeting in-market buyers. Platforms now allow RV and powersports dealers to showcase real units with pricing and photos directly in search results, shortening the path from discovery to conversion.

Over 90% of vehicle and RV purchases begin with online search behavior, which makes visibility across Google, social and marketplaces critical to driving consistent leads and sales.

Seasonality, handled
Side-by-sides before hunting season, watercraft during peak summer demand, motorcycles ahead of riding season. Marketing dollars deployed at the right time with the right message.
Authenticity, measured
Dealers that showcase real inventory, real riders, and real experiences consistently outperform those relying on generic ads. Geofencing, behavioral targeting and AI-driven lead capture make sure you are not just visible, but chosen.
