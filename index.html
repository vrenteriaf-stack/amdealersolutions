Our process

How We Get to
Measurable Results

Every engagement follows the same sequence. Each step produces something the dealership can see: a baseline, a decision, a rebuilt campaign, a tighter process, a number.

  1. Step 01 Discovery and audit We review current spend, vendor agreements, website performance and CRM activity to establish a baseline.
  2. Step 02 Vendor and technology review Providers, applications and platforms are evaluated as one ecosystem, then renegotiated, replaced or removed.
  3. Step 03 Inventory-led strategy Google, Meta and Vehicle Listing Ad campaigns are rebuilt around actual inventory and sales priorities.
  4. Step 04 CRM and follow-up Lead response, appointment and follow-up processes are tightened so the opportunities created actually convert.
  5. Step 05 Sold-customer reporting Matchback reporting ties spend to sold vehicles, and the plan is adjusted from what actually sells.

What does AM Dealer Solutions do?

We help automotive, RV and powersports dealers sell more with tailored, result driven marketing. We combine campaign execution with consulting, and we bridge the gap between advertising and actual sales rather than only reporting on media.

What results do dealers see in the first 60 days?

In the first 60 days our dealers average a 32.45% increase in leads, a 28.71% increase in sales and 38.60% saved in wasted ad spend.

What kinds of dealerships do you work with?

Franchise dealers, independent dealers and service and repair departments on the automotive side, plus RV and powersport dealerships. Each has its own strategy, campaign mix and reporting.

We on average save the dealer 15%-25% on new vendor agreements. We audit third-party listing providers and platforms as one ecosystem, then renegotiate, replace or remove them.
